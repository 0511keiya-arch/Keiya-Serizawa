### Presentation Summary / 発表概要

**English:**  
A poster presentation at WCSPT 2026 evaluating the acute effects of lateral band walk exercises on dynamic knee valgus and dynamic landing biomechanics (kinematics and ground reaction forces) using 3D motion capture (Vicon) and force plates (AMTI).
